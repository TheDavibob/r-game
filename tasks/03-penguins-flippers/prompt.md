Meet the Palmer penguins! `penguins` holds measurements of 344 penguins of three
species (Adélie, Chinstrap and Gentoo) from three islands in the Palmer Archipelago,
Antarctica.

**Recreate the target:** a scatter plot with flipper length (`flipper_length_mm`) on
the x axis and body mass (`body_mass_g`) on the y axis.

You can explore the data first with `head(penguins)` or `names(penguins)`, but the
last line of your code must be the plot.
